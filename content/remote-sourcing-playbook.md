# Remote Sourcing Playbook (India-based, US/EU targets)

Research date: 2026-07-31, three-agent sweep (boards, agencies, US-startup conversion tactics). This feeds the pipeline runbook's intake step.

## The core problem

"Remote" on US boards means three different things: remote US-only, remote region-locked, and remote worldwide. Never trust the word alone. Red flags that exclude India: "US work authorization", "US-based candidates only", "W2", named US timezone with no overlap language. Green flags: "Worldwide" or "Anywhere" tags, EOR/Deel/Remote.com mentioned, a timezone band wide enough for IST, visible non-US teammates on LinkedIn.

## Boards, ranked for this profile

1. **Wellfound** (wellfound.com/remote): startup-native, explicit Worldwide toggle, remote policy stated in listing body. Best narrative fit with the YC story.
2. **Himalayas** (himalayas.app/jobs/worldwide/...): the /worldwide/ URL segment removes ambiguity, has a real salary filter (hard-cut at $100k) and a timezone filter.
3. **web3.career** (+ cryptojobslist.com/remote, cryptocurrencyjobs.co): crypto hires globally by default; lowest filtering overhead and direct fit with Beans and Keom/0VIX evidence.
4. **We Work Remotely** (weworkremotely.com/100-percent-remote-jobs): use the "Anywhere in the World" category specifically, not "Remote".
5. **Remotive** (remotive.com): human-curated, "Worldwide" tag trustworthy, low noise.
6. **RemoteOK** (remoteok.com/remote-jobs-worldwide): biggest volume, use +worldwide URL combos, skim daily.

Also: HN Who's Hiring via hnhiring.com/locations/remote (read each entry; "REMOTE (US)" is a hard exclude), LinkedIn with "work from anywhere" keyword searches.

## Converting "Remote (US)" listings anyway (the WaS problem)

It happens, and the pattern is consistent: early-stage startups block on payroll/compliance overhead, not desire. The lowest-friction ask is B2B contractor (Sujin invoices directly, paid via Deel/Wise, zero setup on their side). EOR is the ask for later-stage companies already using Deel.

- Best-odds segments: crypto/web3 first (distributed by default), then devtools, AI infra, open-source companies (e.g. Supabase-style remote-first cultures).
- Company signals worth 2 minutes per target: careers page says "remote-first" / "async" / "global team"; LinkedIn shows existing non-US engineers; YC remote proxies: remotehub.io/remote-yc-companies, trueup.io/yc/remote.
- Salary reality: anchor at 40-65% of the listed US base as a contractor (US listing $150k-180k → target $70k-100k). Real premium over India market, real discount to US onshore; companies split the savings.

### Location-preempt line (one line, appended to outreach or cover letter, never a paragraph)

I'm based in India but work US-overlapping hours, and I'm set up as an independent contractor, invoiced directly, paid via Deel or Wise, so there's no visa or payroll setup on your end.

## Agencies and talent networks, this-week priority

1. **Braintrust** (usebraintrust.com): talent keeps 100% of rate, client pays the fee on top; enterprise clients; best payout math of anything reviewed.
2. **Toptal**: 3-8 week vetting, ~3% acceptance, but proven $100k+ path; start the clock now.
3. **Arc.dev**: largest India pool, India-calibrated process, no upfront fee, fast to apply.
4. **Gun.io**: ~$150/hr average client bill, ~13-day matching, fast signal.
5. **Lemon.io**: 24-48hr matching once vetted; only worth it at the $80-90/hr band, refuse the $40-45 floor.

Apply-later tier: A.Team (invite-only, slow), Andela (12-month client minimum slows placement), Turing (opaque ~45-50% platform margin, negotiate payout first), Mercor (high rates but unstable projects and invasive monitoring app).

Skip: Uplers (sub-$25/hr mill), Crossover (reputation/process complaints), micro1 (avg $38/hr, too low).

## LinkedIn recruiter outreach

Target boutique US-facing staffing recruiters, not big Indian IT-services shops. Search: "Technical Recruiter Remote/Global", "Talent Partner - Remote Engineering", boolean (recruiter OR "talent partner") AND India AND ("US clients" OR "remote hiring"). Also talent partners at Toptal/Lemon.io/Arc who refer outside their funnel. Use the locked connection-note format; recruiter closer applies.
