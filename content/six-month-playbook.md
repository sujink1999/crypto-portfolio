# Six-Month Playbook (Aug 2026 - Jan 2027)

Synthesized 2026-08-01 from a four-agent research sweep: (1) remote job markets, (2) freelance/contract inflow, (3) content strategy, (4) interview conversion. This is the strategic layer. Operational detail lives in:

- `content/pipeline-runbook.md`: per-company application pipeline
- `content/remote-sourcing-playbook.md`: boards, agencies, contractor conversion
- `content/outreach-playbook.md`: delivery sequence, templates

## Goals

1. **By ~Oct 1 (2 months)**: a signed remote job, $80-150k, contractor or EOR. Anywhere-remote; US-via-contractor, crypto-native, and AI seed-stage are the three fast lanes.
2. **By ~Feb 1 (6 months)**: steady freelance/contract inflow (crypto + AI), so income does not depend on one employer.

## Strategic conclusions (from research)

- **Market ranking for speed**: crypto-native global-remote (1-2 week loops, no payroll friction, $80-180k) > US-remote via contractor/EOR ($80-150k, 38-50 day loops at startups) > AI application startups (fast loops, days to 3 weeks at seed) > devtools/SaaS > Singapore/EU direct employment (slow, legal friction; only viable as contractor-to-foreign-entity, which is the same play as US).
- **Singapore is not a separate lane.** No visa-free employment path; SG companies hiring an India contractor is legally identical to the US contractor play. Do not spend dedicated effort there.
- **The pitch-page + direct-outreach machine stays the primary channel.** Boards and agencies are supplementary intake, not the main lever. Direct hiring-manager outreach: 15-25% reply. Cold portal: 2-5%.
- **Content is a multiplier, not a channel, inside the 2-month window.** Realistic time to inbound from content is 4-6 weeks of consistency. Its job now: make outreach land harder and seed the month 3-6 freelance funnel.
- **The content engine already exists** (found 2026-08-02): a full content-ops system in `~/Development/Vanta/vanta-court` (IG research corpus, trial-reel strategy, scheduler, reel-editor, 3 reels/day timeline) plus the earlier strategy layer in `~/Development/Vanta/personal-brand`. The brand lane is a routing and positioning problem, not a production problem. Full content decisions in the "Content system" section below.
- **Freelance inflow is brand-led, not bounty-led** (Sujin's call, 2026-08-02): no competing for bounties. The play is senior positioning: visible proof of work + productized offers + a consistent public presence, so protocols and startups come inbound. Superteam is used as a network and distribution surface (community, visibility to protocols posting work), not as a bounty grind.
- **"LLM eval harness setup" is genuine white space** as a productized offer: a named 2026 pain point with almost no packaged "hire someone to do this" services. Sujin already builds these (Vanta OS eval harness).
- **The pitch-page tactic itself has no found viral precedent.** It is a novel meta-story and the highest-leverage single content asset.

### Interview conversion rules (from research)
- **Timezone**: preempt on every first call with a named window: "I work 7-10am PT overlap (7:30-10:30pm IST) for standups and pairing, async the rest." Never vague "I'm flexible." Ask what cadence actually needs coverage.
- **Location (2026-08-07)**: never volunteered. Forced profile/application fields say "Bali, Indonesia" (nomadic, true). If it comes up live: "I'm in India for a bit." Tax/KYC/contracts stay India exactly. Full rules in remote-sourcing-playbook.md.
- **Vanta**: locked framing, "I co-founded Vanta," past tense. Do not volunteer detail in early screens; disclose honestly near offer or if asked directly. Expect an IP carve-out addendum, not a rejection. One-liner if asked early: "I co-founded a startup this year; it has its own team and I'm not operationally full-time on it. Happy to walk through specifics for IP paperwork."
- **AI in interviews**: ask the recruiter during scheduling whether AI assistance is allowed or expected (normal question in 2026). Prep both modes. Never let a tool answer a follow-up you cannot defend line by line.
- **Speed**: ask every recruiter for the loop-to-offer timeline on call one; request same-week bundled rounds; once at final round anywhere, tell every other active recruiter and ask them to accelerate; once any offer lands, broadcast a 2-week decision deadline to all pending processes.
- **Negotiation without competing offers**: cite levels.fyi-style comp data, cite shipped evidence (Mudrex 10M, real TVL), move sign-on/equity before base. Contractor rate = quote 15-25% above equivalent salary to offset no benefits. Anchor at 40-65% of listed US base per the sourcing playbook.
- **Tax notes**: Section 44ADA presumptive taxation (declare 50% of receipts as profit, gross up to Rs 75L, ITR-4). Foreign startup equity: RBI OPI rules, Schedule FA disclosure, perquisite tax at exercise. Know these before saying yes to an equity-heavy offer.

## Month 3-6: build the inflow machine

- **Job is the base layer.** The freelance goal assumes the job is manageable alongside; scope monthly hours honestly.
- **Brand is the engine.** By month 3 the content cadence has run 8+ weeks, past the documented 4-6 week threshold to predictable inbound. The system: every post is proof of work (demo clips, "how I built X", the pitch-page meta-story), every post routes to the offer pages, and replies into founder threads put the work in front of people who can buy it. Consistency beats virality; one strong post a week for 6 months outperforms a burst.
- **Warm outreach as ignition, framed as offers, not asks**: brand-led does not mean purely passive in months 3-4. A handful of researched messages/week to funded protocols and AI startups, each a specific observation about their product plus a link to the relevant offer page, reads as a senior peer opening a conversation. Documented pattern: specificity beats volume (83 personalized emails, 5 clients, $6,400 in 28 days). Sequence: email day 1, LinkedIn day 3, follow-up day 7. Once inbound covers capacity, stop.
- **Grants as upside, not pipeline**: monitor Ethereum Foundation ESP wishlists/RFPs and Solana Foundation grants (milestone-based, $5k-200k); apply only on a strong stack match. Solana Mobile Builder Grants fit the React Native background. Base Builder Grants are retroactive. Skip Dework and Passionfroot.
- **Referral loop**: after each delivery, ask the client for one intro to another funded team. Clients 1-2 come from warm outreach and inbound; clients 3+ come from referrals and content compounding.

## Content system (settled 2026-08-02, from the positioning session + Sujin's own IG data)

### Platform roles
- **Instagram (@sujinkingselin / @buildwithsujin, ~727 followers)**: grows Vanta's future consumer audience. Runs on the existing vanta-court engine — NOT autopilot: every reel is script → Sujin's approval → shoot → render → schedule, and shoots are the real energy limit (max 3/day). Budget it honestly. NOT the money engine for this plan.
- **X**: where the buyers are (founders, CTOs, hiring managers). Gets you hired now and contracted later. The incremental daily attention goes here.
- **LinkedIn**: makes recruiters comfortable. Same stories as carousels (best-engaging format, ~6.6%).

### Positioning: one character everywhere
"An engineer who builds AI systems and points them at his own body." Not two niches (AI + health), one character containing both. Sujin's simplest formulation (2026-08-02, adopted as the operating test): **the account proves two things — great dev, and jacked.** On X, lead with craft and AI systems, never founder-grind framing ("Vanta update week 12" reads as a founder who will leave; "how I built X" reads as craft). Health appears as evidence and experiments, never as identity or journey.

### The reel test (decides every post)
**Does this reel make someone think "this guy can build" or "this guy is fit"? If neither, it does not get made.** Equivalent framing: teaching or experimenting, with my own artifact as the receipt. Kills: generic health advice, third-person explainers (2026-08-01 follow-count audit: 0-2 follows each, every time), lifestyle/day-N updates without baseline data, AI news commentary, tool listicles, Vanta product pitches (his worst format, proven 19-600 views), fitness technique coaching (no authority), quote-card/aesthetic-only filler (the @jazzmattaazz failure mode: years of consistent, well-produced, hook-less commodity content bought ~2k followers).

### What his data proved (50-reel self-study + 2026-08-01 follow-count audit)
- **Follows-per-view is the only judging metric.** Per-reel follow counts are in-app only (API rejects the `follows` metric for reels — tested); Sujin logs them by hand into the vanta-court DB and the weekly review computes follows/1k.
- Build/AI proof content is the growth engine and best follow converter (website demo 6.3 follows/1k, "claude edited this" 5.1/1k). The "Claude edits my videos" lane is confirmed open by competitor research (forseth.ai runs it daily on a small account).
- Third-person explainers convert at 0-1.4 follows/1k regardless of shares (sleepmaxing: 58 shares, 3 follows). Borrowed knowledge only ships with Sujin's own receipt on screen (the hunterhine_ move: generic tips over HIS live sleep-score).
- Experiments spike at start and end; middles die. A day-1 works only when the baseline itself is a receipt (his ugly real data on screen, countoncasey pattern) — never a bare "I'm starting X" (announcement-class reels are the account's two worst performers: 446 and 1,041 views). Fix for middles: compress series to natural beats (baseline-receipt start, 2-3 surprise milestones, finale with full data) and make each episode self-contained. Experiments without a public commitment (e.g. the gut protocol) are documented privately — data + clips logged daily — and published retrospectively as "this is how I did it" receipt drops.
- News-jacking is a lottery (77k views, 29 follows = 0.37/1k). Views without follows is the trap.
- Volume is a real lever ONLY with identity + receipt in every reel (@eabhaom posts 5-6/day but her breakouts are the two long first-person confessionals with concrete numbers; her ~7k/week follower jump likely rides an off-reel webinar funnel — do not benchmark reel-only growth against funnel-driven accounts; the ManyChat pipe is our version of that lever when ready).

### Why people follow (the model)
A follow is a bet on future posts, not payment for the current one. Three drivers: an unfinished story (serialized experiments with stakes and a countdown), a character they want more of, and a reliable stream of one utility. Comment-gates ("comment SYSTEM") are a conversion layer, not loyalty. One-off value earns likes and saves, never follows.

### Reel types (the rotation)
Build lane: (1) "how I built X" walkthroughs 40-60s, the proven banger; (2) AI-system reveals (Claude edits my videos, AI posts this account); (3) silent wrong/right demos under 10s (cheapest reach fisher, works muted); (4) receipt-first teaching using the wrong-right-cue-drill skeleton on his own work.
Health lane: (1) serialized AI experiments ("I gave Claude my sleep data and 30 days"), the follow engine and moat, one live at a time — day-1 requires his real baseline numbers on screen, and uncommitted experiments run privately and publish as retrospective receipt drops; (2) number-forward contrarian takes (Rs 4,000 recovery kit vs what actually moved my HRV); (3) funny/relatable engineer-body moments, the health reach fisher.
Health rule: n=1 experiment framing ("here is what I did and what the data showed"), never prescription. Respect the do-not-post rules in vanta-court about.md.

### Trials vs grid
Everything posts as trials first (standing rule). Trials can be wild and cheap but each needs a hypothesis worth acting on; junk-as-filler pollutes the dashboard. Trials are judged on **follows-per-view** (logged from in-app; views alone already lied once at 77k). The grid is earned by numbers only. Recreates of proven viral reels fill extra slots at zero scripting cost: recreate the mechanic, wear his skin (skip if it would make sense from a non-engineer account), and dedup-check against his own catalog first (IG matches concepts, not pixels; one idea gets one reel ever).

### Operating rhythm
- One weekly planning day covering all verticals; the other six days are execution with zero deciding.
- Max 3 shoots/day (real energy limit). Each shoot yields three cuts: IG trial reel, X native video with a builder caption, LinkedIn carousel. Plan stories, not per-platform posts.
- X replies remain the only non-repurposable work: 10-15/day into founder and hiring-manager threads. **Hand-typed only, never tooled, ramp gently** — the account already caught (and shed) a spam flag from reply volume in July 2026; the vanta-court reply automation stays archived.
- Pipeline-building happens on the planning day only, or when a manual step provably burns daily time. Until the job is signed, engineering hours belong to the job pipeline; automation ideas mid-week are procrastination in disguise.

### Backlog (deliberately not started)
- Funded-startup tracker: weekly sweep of funding announcements (TechCrunch, YC batches, crypto raise feeds) resolved to founder X handles, producing a daily engage-list; hot companies graduate into pitch-page targets. Good idea, assist-not-automation (replies stay handwritten), build after the job signs or on a planning day.
- Offer validation: the two productized offers are researched hypotheses, not proven demand. Every warm-outreach reply is market research; expect to rewrite the offers once by month 4.

## Weekly operating rhythm (steady state)

- Mon-Fri morning: pipeline (job search) or client work (post-hire).
- Daily 30-60 min: content per the cadence above.
- Fri: review the week. Applications sent, replies, page views (beacon), interviews scheduled, content follows-per-view by lane. Feed learnings back into the playbooks (own beacon data beats published benchmarks).
- Weekly planning day: plan the week's content stories across all verticals (IG/X/LinkedIn cuts per shoot), refill the clip pool, and do any pipeline automation work. No content deciding on other days.
- **One content bet per week** (settled 2026-08-01): the planning day names ONE hypothesis ("this week: AI-system demo reels"), the whole week's slate runs in that lane, Friday's review judges it on follows-per-reel and calls keep / kill / double-down. One variable at a time; no mid-week pivots.

## Kill criteria and pivots

- If by week 4 reply rate on outreach is under ~10%, revisit targeting (segment mix, seniority of targets) before changing the message; crypto and devtools convert best.
- If by week 6 there are zero active interview loops, double the crypto-native share of the pipeline (fastest loops) and raise agency effort (Lemon.io/Arc.dev matches).
- If an offer lands below $80k, take it only if contractor-structured and compatible with freelance building; the 6-month plan makes the floor temporary.
- If the job lands early (weeks 3-5), immediately reallocate pipeline hours to the offer pages and doubling the content cadence.
- If by month 4 content has produced zero inbound leads, the fix is distribution, not more production: increase reply volume into bigger accounts, and add 5-10 warm offer-framed messages/week until the flywheel starts.
