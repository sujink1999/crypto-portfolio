# Inbound Surface Playbook (profiles that make companies come to you)

Research date: 2026-08-07, three-agent sweep (inbound marketplaces, channel conversion rates, recruiters/agencies). Complements content/remote-sourcing-playbook.md (outbound boards) and the six-month playbook. Goal: maximize passive surface area so recruiters/founders reach out, without eating pipeline hours.

## Conversion reality (what the data says)

Data quality note: most published numbers are SEO-recycled aggregations; the two solid sources are CNBC (Jan 2025, LinkedIn OTW data) and Pragmatic Engineer (2026 market reports). Treat everything else as order-of-magnitude.

- Referral: 10-20x the interview rate of cold apply. Highest-leverage channel that exists.
- Direct targeted hiring-manager outreach: 5-15% reply realistically (the 15-25% figures come from vendor blogs). The pitch-page channel sits here, and personalization pushes toward the top of the band. No published benchmark exists for the custom-page tactic itself; it predates measurement.
- Cold portal apply: 0.1-2% raw; 2-9% if the resume is tailored. 52-61% of employers now AI-screen resumes, so JD-literal keyword matching (already a project rule) matters more than ever, and volume-spray degrades faster.
- LinkedIn Open-To-Work, recruiters-only mode: recruiter outreach response 14.5% vs 4.6% without the signal (CNBC 2025). Public green banner adds contact volume but carries a desperation-read risk; use recruiters-only.
- Reverse marketplaces publish no candidate-side funnel data. Assume modest inbound; the cost is only setup time.
- Interview funnel benchmark: ~75% pass HR screen, ~25% of those pass tech screen, ~3 onsites per offer.

Ranking implication: referral > targeted outreach with the page > marketplace inbound > cold apply. Inbound surfaces are a background layer, not a replacement for the pipeline.

## Tier 1: set up this week (confirmed alive, India-eligible, real inbound mechanism)

1. **LinkedIn** — turn on Open-To-Work in recruiters-only mode. Keyword-optimize headline/about for recruiter search (recruiters' primary search surface; the badge without keywords does little). Set remote/worldwide preferences explicitly.
2. **Wellfound** — complete profile + "open to offers". AI sourcing pitches complete profiles with real work links to founders. The YC (Mudrex W19) story lands best here. Their paid "Featured" spotlight (8 weeks) reportedly 3x recruiter connections; the one quantified paid boost found. Consider it if free inbound is thin after 2 weeks.
3. **YC Work at a Startup** — free profile, founders reach out directly, employer pool is YC-vetted. The Mudrex YC affiliation resonates most with exactly this audience. ~20 min.
4. **Superteam Talent** (talent.superteam.fun) — Solana-backed, India-origin network, curated matching. Likely the highest-signal crypto channel for an India-based DeFi builder. Aligns with the existing plan to join Superteam India for network, not bounties.
5. **web3.career talent profile + cryptojobslist.com + cryptocurrencyjobs.co Talent Collective** — three crypto talent listings, near-zero marginal effort after the first (same content). cryptocurrencyjobs.co actively pushes matches.
6. **Mercor** — explicitly India-friendly, AI interview (~20 min), matches to AI labs/enterprises at $80-150/hr contract (can exceed the salary target on hourly basis). AI/LLM evidence (Vanta OS eval harness) fits their demand. Note from earlier research: monitoring app on some projects is invasive; evaluate per engagement.
7. **micro1** — same AI-interview model, lower bar, wider net, $40-90/hr typical. Earlier research said skip for low rates; as a zero-effort parallel profile it costs nothing, but never accept the low band.
8. **Peerlist** — India-heavy "proof of work" profile, GitHub/product integrations, free. May skew India-domestic roles; low downside.
9. **GitHub profile** — pinned showcase repos + profile README pointing at sujin.tech. Baseline hygiene, keep current.

## Tier 2: recruiters working for you

- **Paraform recruiters** — 10,000+ independent recruiters on placement bounties (raised $40M Mar 2026), financially motivated to place fast. No self-serve candidate intake; the move is direct LinkedIn outreach to individual Paraform recruiters in crypto/AI/full-stack niches (search "Paraform recruiter"). Use the locked connection-note discipline, recruiter persona.
- **Crypto recruiting firms** — register as candidate: Plexus (plexusrs.com, crypto specialist since 2017), CryptoRecruit (cryptorecruit.com, APAC roots so India-friendlier). Mine echoglobal.tech "22 Crypto Recruiters 2026" list for more names on a planning day.
- **AI-startup recruiters** — Recruits Lab (venture-backed AI startups, 14-day avg time-to-offer): worth a direct LinkedIn outreach to their recruiters.
- **Boutique US-facing staffing recruiters on LinkedIn** — per remote-sourcing-playbook.md search strings; unchanged.
- What makes any of them prioritize you: complete keyword-rich profile, explicit rate band and availability ("available now"), real work links, and fast replies (slow responders get routed around).

## Tier 3: maybe / verify before spending time

- **Hired.com** — alive but US/UK/EU employer skew; India inbound unverified.
- **Otta / Welcome to the Jungle** — merged, Euro-centric; cheap to set up, uncertain payoff.
- **Cord.co, Underdog.io, Hackajob** — alive but India/worldwide policy unverified; 5-minute manual checks before any effort.
- **A.Team** — very senior/specialized fit is good, India eligibility unconfirmed, invite-only slow.
- **Contra** — real but slow: user reports say inbound starts ~month 4-5 with endorsements. Fits the month 2-3 freelance-seeding phase (already in the playbook), not the 2-month job window.
- **Go Fractional** — application-based fractional network; fractional-CTO positioning is a later play, not now.

## Operating notes

- Every profile links to sujin.tech and, where fields allow, embeds the same evidence set (Mudrex 10M downloads, Vanta OS, Beans/Keom). Vanta framing rule applies: "I co-founded Vanta", never present-tense.
- Location rule (2026-08-07): never volunteer location; set worldwide-remote preferences. Where a platform forces a location field, use "Bali, Indonesia" (presentation layer; nomadic and true). KYC/payout/tax details stay India, exactly. Pair with the contractor-preempt line from remote-sourcing-playbook.md.
- Batch the setup: Tier 1 is roughly one focused day (profiles share the same source copy). Agencies and recruiter outreach ride the existing week-1 clocks.
- Review inbound volume at the Friday review; kill or Feature-boost surfaces based on actual DMs received, not vibes.
